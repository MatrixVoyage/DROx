Consolidated Research & Operational Limitations (Milestone 13.5)

1. Facial Reenactment Boundary: Subtle expression manipulations (Face2Face) operate inside the natural human facial subspace without boundary seams, yielding ~38%–43% sensitivity compared to 89%–100% on Deepfakes and NeuralTextures.
2. Handcrafted Branch Gating: Standalone frequency and texture branches produce elevated false alarms (28%–47% on clean reals); their noisy anomaly signals are gated/suppressed by the multi-domain fusion head.
3. Threshold Sensitivity Trade-Off: Standard threshold (τ=0.50) achieves 93.88% clean specificity; high-sensitivity operating point (τ*=0.0200) increases Face2Face recall to 43.10% while accepting a 6.12% false alarm rate.
4. Cross-Domain Generalization: Cross-domain transfer (Celeb-DF v2 ~0.796 AUC) exhibits domain-shift degradation relative to in-domain seen synthesis (>0.98 AUC).
5. Probabilistic Confidence Semantics: Model scores reflect statistical confidence within the validated distribution, not objective physical proof or sole legal evidence.
